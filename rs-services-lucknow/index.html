01 Services

Everything your air
conditioner needs.

From a unit that stopped cooling overnight to a scheduled deep clean before summer, RS Services handles the full range of day-to-day AC work for homes and businesses in Lucknow.

  • 01 AC Repair Fault finding and repair for units that are not cooling, tripping the breaker, dripping water, or making unusual noise.
  • 02 Servicing & Deep Cleaning Foam and coil cleaning, filter and blower wash, drain line clearing, and a cooling performance check afterwards.
  • 03 Installation & Shifting New split AC installation, uninstallation when you move, and re-installation with proper mounting, levelling and pipe work.
  • 04 Gas Filling & Leak Repair Refrigerant top-up for low cooling, pressure testing on the cooling circuit, and sealing the leak that caused the loss.
  • 05 Window AC Service Servicing, repair and installation for window air conditioners, including tray cleaning and drainage correction.
  • 06 Preventive Maintenance Routine servicing visits for flats, shops and offices, scheduled around you so cooling does not fail in the hottest weeks.
